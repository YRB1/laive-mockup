Orders

When does Drop 01 go live?
Drop 01 opens on 05.12.26 at 18:00 GMT. Sign up to the list to get early access 24 hours before.
Can I change or cancel my order?
Email us within 1 hour of ordering and we'll do our best. Once an order is packed it can't be changed.
Will you restock?
Drops are limited runs. Some core pieces may return in new colours. Join the list to hear first.

Delivery

How much is delivery?
UK standard (2–4 working days): £3.95, free over £75. UK next day: £5.95. Europe: from £9.95. Rest of world: from £14.95.
Do you ship internationally?
Yes, to most countries. Duties and taxes for orders outside the UK are calculated at checkout.
How do I track my order?
You'll get a tracking link by email as soon as your order ships.

Returns

What is your returns policy?
Free returns within 30 days of delivery for UK orders. Items must be unworn with tags attached.
How long do refunds take?
Refunds are processed within 5 working days of your return reaching us.

Sizing

Size guide
How do the oversized tees fit?
Boxy and dropped-shoulder. Take your usual size for the intended oversized fit, or size down for a regular fit.

Payments

What payment methods do you accept?
Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal and Klarna (pay in 3).
Is checkout secure?
Yes. Checkout runs on Shopify with secure, encrypted payments.
